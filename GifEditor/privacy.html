Privacy Policy for GIF Frame Master

Last updated: October 3, 2026

GIF Frame Master respects your privacy and is fully committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines how our Chrome extension operates regarding user data.

1. Data Collection and Storage

GIF Frame Master operates entirely locally on your device. All GIF editing operations—including frame extraction, cropping, resizing, merging, and optimization via WebAssembly—take place directly inside your browser.

We do not collect, store, transmit, log, or share any personal data, user files, images, or browsing history. Your files never leave your computer.

2. Permissions

The extension requests only the minimum permissions strictly necessary to execute local file reading and interface rendering. No background data tracking or external telemetry is performed.

3. Third-Party Services

This extension does not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or remote advertising frameworks. All processing libraries are bundled locally within the extension package.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected directly on this page.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy or the extension, you can reach out via GitHub: @cruelben.
